# Blomstr

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A backoffice workspace for organizing content projects, collaborating, reviewing versions, and preparing publishing. Preserve the existing workflows and permissions during visual work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing implementation includes projects, notes, files, mindmaps, reviews, publishing preparation, tasks, calendar, team and workspace settings. Automatic publishing is not connected; do not imply that it is. Audience positioning beyond the existing team workflow remains undecided.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Blomstr name, flower logo, primary green #49C470 and Geist. The user approved a sitewide Linear-inspired frontend with Apple-like precision: restrained, functional, responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile. No excessive glass, decorative gradients, oversized cards or unnecessary animation.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action clear without inventing new product capabilities.
- Preserve content, access rules, routes and familiar workflows.
- Keep visual changes reversible and separate from backend work.
